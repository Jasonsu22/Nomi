Why Nomi

Lectures move fast. Nomi helps you keep up.

Studying in a second language is hard enough. Nomi takes care of the listening and note-taking, so you can focus on understanding.

The teacher talks fast

Accents, speed and long sentences make it easy to miss a point and lose the thread.

Live text appears as they speak. Missed something? Scroll back and read it.

New words everywhere

Technical terms and idioms slow you down when you are still thinking in your first language.

Each passage is translated right under the original, so you learn both.

Listening or writing?

Taking notes while you listen means you do both badly. Rewatching recordings takes hours.

Get the full transcript, a summary, and answers to your questions.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

1

Start recording

Click Start Transcription, pick your microphone, the lecture language and your language.

2

Follow along

Read the live text and translation while you listen. Light words are still being heard; dark words are final.

FAQ

Questions students ask

Which Macs does Nomi run on?

Nomi needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later.

Do I need internet in class?

No. After you download the models once, transcription and translation work offline. Internet is only needed if you turn on Web search or use an API model for questions.

Which languages are supported?

Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Arabic, both for the lecture and for the translation.

Can I chat with Nomi while it is recording?

Yes. Ask questions during class without stopping the recording. Live transcription keeps priority, so the text keeps up with the teacher.

Can Nomi find something from an earlier class?

Yes. Ask something like “what did the teacher say about the exam last week?” and Nomi searches all your lectures, earlier chats and files, then shows where each part of the answer came from.

Does Nomi remember things about me?

Only what makes answers better: the preferences, courses and goals it learns from your chats. It stays on your Mac, and you can edit, pin, clear or turn it off in Settings › Memory.

Is it okay to record my lectures?

Rules differ between schools and countries. Check your university's policy and ask your teacher before you record a class.

How much does Nomi cost?

Nomi is open source under the MIT license. If you connect your own API for questions, that provider may charge you.
