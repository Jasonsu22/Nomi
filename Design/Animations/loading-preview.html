Nomi loading leaf

The Nomi leaf, live. Every shape is drawn from the real logo outline: it is sampled into points, and the animation bends those points from one shape into the next. The leaf grows out of a seed, turns into a spark, a page and a drop, then comes back home.

 

E · Shape-shifter

One leaf that never sits still: seed, leaf, spark, page, drop, leaf. It squashes a little at each change and spins a quarter turn into the spark, the way Claude Code's star flickers. In the app each step could settle on its own shape: a page while reading files, a spark while thinking, a drop while searching, the leaf while writing.
