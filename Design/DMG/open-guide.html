第一次打开 NomiOpening Nomi for the first time

Nomi 是免费应用，还没有在 Apple 登记，所以 macOS 会请你确认一次。只需要做一次。 Nomi is free and not yet registered with Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. You only do this once.

  1. 1
    把 Nomi 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。 Drag Nomi into the Applications folder.
  2. 2
    在「应用程序」里打开 Nomi。看到「无法验证」的提示时，点 完成。 Open Nomi from Applications. When macOS says it could not verify Nomi, click Done.
  3. 3
    点下面的按钮，打开「隐私与安全性」。 Click the button below to open Privacy & Security. 打开隐私与安全性 · Open Privacy & Security
  4. 4
    向下滚动，找到关于 Nomi 的提示，点 仍要打开，再用密码或触控 ID 确认。 Scroll down to the message about Nomi, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
「仍要打开」只会在第 2 步之后出现大约一小时。没看到的话，再打开一次 Nomi，然后重新点上面的按钮。 “Open Anyway” appears only after step 2, for about an hour. If you don't see it, open Nomi once more and click the button again.
